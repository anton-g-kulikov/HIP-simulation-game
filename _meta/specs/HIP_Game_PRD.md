# PRD --- High Impact Professionals (HIP)

## Vision

A mobile-first daily strategy game that teaches players how career
capital compounds over time.

Players do not control conversations, interviews, or negotiations. They
make a single decision each day:

> **Where should I invest my limited career energy today?**

The game simulates the downstream consequences over an entire career.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Loop

Every real day:

1.  Review outcomes from previous decisions.
2.  Receive new opportunities.
3.  Allocate a fixed Energy budget across available investments.
4.  End the turn.

The simulation advances automatically until the next day.

Average session: **2--5 minutes**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Time Compression

The game intentionally changes temporal resolution.

## Level 1 --- Exploitation

Age: 28--29

Resolution: **1 turn = 1 month**

Turns: **12**

Goal: Extract maximum value from the player's existing career capital.

Typical investments: - Current job - Promotions - Job applications -
Learning - Portfolio - Network - Personal brand

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Level 2 --- Exploration

Age: 29--32

Resolution: **1 turn = 1 quarter**

Turns: **12**

Goal: Evaluate larger career pivots.

Examples: - Change industry - Join startup - AI safety - Policy -
Nonprofit - Found a company - Graduate education

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Level 3 --- Leverage

Age: 32--44

Resolution: **1 turn = 1 year**

Turns: **12**

Goal: Deploy accumulated career capital toward large-scale impact.

Examples: - Build an organization - Lead a research program - Enter
government - Become executive - Scale a startup - Allocate funding -
Mentor future leaders

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Total campaign:

36 real days.

Represents approximately sixteen years of a career.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Resource

Energy.

Every turn the player receives a fixed Energy budget.

Actions consume Energy.

Repeated actions become temporarily more expensive until they cool down,
encouraging diversified investment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Persistent Pipelines

## Hiring

Role discovery → Application → Interviews → Offer → Acceptance

Outputs: - Compensation - Prestige - Experience - New opportunities

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Current Employer

Extra effort → Visible impact → Recognition → Promotion

Outputs: - Reputation - Salary - Leadership experience

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Learning

Learning → Practice → Competency → Specialization

Outputs: - New opportunities - Better conversion in relevant careers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Portfolio

Idea → Build → Launch → Results

Outputs: - Demonstrated capability - Founder potential - Case studies

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Personal Brand

Publish → Audience → Recognition → Inbound opportunities

Outputs: - Recruiter outreach - Speaking invitations - Advisory work

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Network

Relationships → Trust → Referrals

Outputs: - Hidden opportunities - Warm introductions - Mentorship

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Financial Capital

Compensation → Savings → Runway → Freedom

Outputs: - Ability to pursue riskier, higher-impact paths

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Events

Random one-off events influence the world but are not repeatable loops.

Examples:

-   Layoffs
-   AI breakthrough
-   Recession
-   Acquisition
-   Family emergency
-   Visa issues
-   Pandemic
-   Major conference
-   Grant announcement

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Philosophy

Players never answer interview questions.

Players never negotiate.

Players never solve puzzles.

The game simulates execution.

Players only allocate scarce resources under uncertainty.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success

The objective is not maximizing salary.

The objective is maximizing expected lifetime impact.

Career outcomes include:

-   Career capital
-   Financial runway
-   Reputation
-   Network
-   Learning
-   Organizations influenced
-   People managed
-   Donations
-   Ventures founded
-   Expected lifetime impact

Multiple career archetypes should be viable, including entrepreneurship,
research, engineering, product, operations, policy, nonprofit
leadership, and earning-to-give.
